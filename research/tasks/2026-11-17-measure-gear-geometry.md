## Goal

Tape-measure the real airframe: main-gear track and wheel width, nose-wheel position, belly
height between the gears, strut positions. The simulated bay (3.5 cm clearance per side, 9 cm
under the belly) comes from a mesh whose scale is provisional.

## Done when

Measurements in a decision record; `geometry.bay()` re-checked against them (a test).
