## Decision

The 2D RL parking stack (`rl/`: `MecanumVecEnv`, PPO teacher/student, `evaluate.py` gate,
export) and the `explore/` hypothesis loop are deleted. They live at tag `pre-pose-20261117`.
Their results are distilled into `research/attempts/` under the roots
`2026-11-17-track-2d-rl` and `2026-11-17-track-explore-loop`. The Pi runtime still carries
`StudentPolicy` and `StagedBaseline`; removing them is a task, because it touches motion code.

## Why

The best student reached 966/1000 in sim at L1 and 0/3 on the car; every open explanation
(plant mismatch, gyro noise, loop lag, the blind finale under the drone) sat below the policy.
Fernando, 2026-11-17: the car does not work well enough for RL to be the next step; work
moves to 3D, model-based perception.

## Revisit if

Pose estimation and a classical parking state machine work on the car, and a learned
controller is needed for something they cannot do.
