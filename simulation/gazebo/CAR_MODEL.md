# Payload Car Model (`payload_car`)

Default simulated car in `simulation/gazebo/generate.py` since 2026-11-27 (replaces the Freenove
FNK0043 platform; see [`docs/archive/2026-11-27-freenove-car.md`](../../docs/archive/2026-11-27-freenove-car.md)
and [`research/decisions/2026-11-27-replace-freenove-car.md`](../../research/decisions/2026-11-27-replace-freenove-car.md)).

## Source CAD & coordinate frame

- **Original CAD:** `simulation/gazebo/assets/car/payload_assembly/payload_assembly.usdz`
  (byte-identical copy of `Payload Assembly.usdz`, SHA-256 recorded in
  `simulation/gazebo/assets/car/payload_assembly/SOURCE.md`).
- **USD quirks fixed by `convert_payload_usd.py`:**
  - `metersPerUnit = 0.01` (centimetres → metres).
  - Stage metadata declares `upAxis = Y`, while the geometry is physically `+Z` up and `+Y` forward
    (`forward___Y__marker__1_`, `M12_lens__1_` at front `+Y`).
  - Raw assembly is off-origin (wheel hub mean at `(-20.00, +20.00, +0.82) cm`, lowest roller
    vertex at `z = -1.174129 cm`).
- **Gazebo model frame** (matching `simulation/gazebo/assembly.py`):
  - `+X` forward (camera/lens), `+Y` left, `+Z` up, SI metres.
  - Origin `(0, 0, 0)` at the centre of the four wheel hubs projected onto ground contact
    (`z = 0` at the lowest roller vertex).
  - Transform: `x_gz = (y_usd - 20.0) * 0.01`, `y_gz = -(x_usd + 20.0) * 0.01`,
    `z_gz = (z_usd + 1.174129) * 0.01` (`Rz(-90 deg)`).

## Visual meshes & camera-truth gate

Generated by `simulation/gazebo/convert_payload_usd.py` from `1,242` meshes (`1,026,269` triangles):

- Exterior ray-visibility culling drops `766` completely hidden internal CAD meshes (leaving `476`
  visible meshes / `346,963` triangles).
- Per-link quadric edge collapse reduces the model to **`52,000` triangles** (`94.9%` removed):
  `chassis.obj` (`24,000` tris) + 4 local-hub wheel meshes (`7,000` tris each), sharing
  `payload_car.mtl` (`40` USD material colours preserved), plus a combined game-ready PBR master
  `payload_car.glb` (`982 KB`).
- Gated against the full `1,026,269`-triangle CAD with `tools/mesh_opt/meshgate.py` (`--max-tris 60000`):
  - **Seed 0 (`PASS`):** `bbox_mm = 0.0349`, `iou_min = 0.9990`, `depth_p99_mm = 0.0479`,
    `seethrough_pct = 0.0019`, `normal_p90_deg = 5.08°`, `vis_to_cand_p99_mm = 0.0770`,
    `cand_to_orig_p99_mm = 0.1310`, `pierced_mm2 = 0.0000`, `dent_mm2 = 0.3751`,
    `backface_mm2 = 0.5627`.
  - **Seed 42 held-out (`PASS`):** `bbox_mm = 0.0349`, `iou_min = 0.9990`, `depth_p99_mm = 0.0479`,

## Usage

```sh
cd simulation/gazebo
python3 generate.py                                          # writes static payload_world.sdf (default)
python3 generate.py --car payload --sample-seed 0 --output /tmp/payload_s0.sdf # drivable PRIOR SAMPLE + .sample.json sidecar
python3 generate.py --drone --output drone_docking_world.sdf # static payload_car + drone
python3 generate.py --room --drone --output lab_docking_world.sdf
python3 generate.py --car freenove                           # archived drivable Freenove model
python3 generate.py --car freenove --drone --output drone_docking_world.sdf
gz sdf -k payload_world.sdf
```

> **Driving / dynamics note:** Until mass and motor parameters are measured, `generate.py` (without
> `--sample-seed`) emits `payload_car` with `<static>true</static>` (geometry/visual and collision
> model only). Every Gazebo drive, control-panel, lockstep, and physics-check tool rejects unmeasured
> `payload_car` worlds with an explicit error. Pass `--car payload --sample-seed N` to draw a
> **prior sample** (explicitly labelled `PRIOR SAMPLE, not measured`), or `--car freenove` to drive
> the archived Freenove model.

## Monte Carlo prior

> **WARNING — PRIOR SAMPLES, NOT A CALIBRATED CAR.** Everything in
> `simulation/gazebo/payload_prior.json` and `simulation/gazebo/payload_montecarlo.json` is an
> explicit uncertainty prior over unmeasured physical parameters, **not** a calibrated vehicle model.
> **No controller tuned on these prior samples may be trusted on physical hardware.**
> **One kitchen-scale weighing plus one S4 floor `probe-axes` run replace this prior**
> (tracked in `research/tasks/2026-11-27-characterise-payload-car.md`).

### Prior construction (`payload_prior.json`)

Every numeric entry in `simulation/gazebo/payload_prior.json` carries an explicit `"source"` tag in
`{"owner", "cad-volume", "datasheet-range-prior"}` (reproducible via `build_payload_prior.py`):

- **Owner upper bound (`"source": "owner"`):** the owner's only physical statement (2026-11-27) is
  that total mass is **"probably under 300g"** (`<= 0.300 kg`).
- **Bottom-up CAD mass, COM, and inertia (`"source": "cad-volume"` + `"datasheet-range-prior"`):**
  extracted from all `1,242` mesh prims in `payload_assembly.usdz` (`1,241` watertight meshes + `1`
  tiny `0.0005 cm³` SBC SMD pad using convex-hull fallback):
  - Structural, wheel, PCB, fastener, and wire CAD volumes × typical material/FDM-infill density
    ranges (`PLA/PETG 450–1270 kg/m³`, `TPU 650–1250 kg/m³`, `FR4 1800–2100 kg/m³`,
    `steel 7750–8050 kg/m³`, `brass 8400–8700 kg/m³`, insulated `copper wire 2500–5500 kg/m³`).
  - Purchased envelope assemblies use typical datasheet mass ranges labelled `"prior"`
    (`2S LiPo 72×35×17 mm`: `30–78 g`; `4× N20 gearmotors`: `9–12 g` each; `BNO055`: `2.5–4.5 g`;
    `40×40 M12 camera`: `8–22 g`; `56×41 SBC`: `15–42 g`).
  - **Untruncated bottom-up total mass** (50,000 draws): median **`334.8 g`**, 5–95%
    **`[287.8, 381.6] g`** (`12.2%` of untruncated draws are `<= 300 g`, showing that solid prints
    plus a heavy LiPo/SBC would exceed `300 g` and reinforcing the need for a scale measurement).
  - **Truncated at owner bound (`<= 0.300 kg`):** median **`290.1 g`**, 5–95%
    **`[270.2, 299.1] g`**.
  - **COM & inertia:** derived per sample from the sampled per-part masses and their CAD centroids
    and polyhedral inertia tensors via the parallel-axis theorem (never a box guess).
- **Drivetrain & contact prior (`"source": "datasheet-range-prior"`):**
  - Discrete uniform prior over 8 common N20 gearmotor variants (`50:1`, `100:1`, `150:1`, `210:1`,
    `298:1` at `6 V` and `50:1`, `100:1`, `150:1` at `12 V`) with datasheet no-load RPM and stall
    torque ranges, driven from a 2S LiPo (`7.4 V` nominal, `7.0–8.2 V` sampled) through an unknown
    driver (`supply_efficiency ∈ [0.65, 0.95]`).
  - Roller friction (`mu ∈ [0.35, 1.10]`), passive bearing damping (`[1e-6, 1e-4] N·m·s/rad`) and
    friction (`[5e-7, 2e-5] N·m`), motor deadband (`[0.0, 0.20]`), PWM exponent (`[0.60, 1.15]`),
    and drive/brake lags (`[0.03, 0.28] s` / `[0.02, 0.18] s`) span the Freenove's measured floor
    values (`docs/L7_FIELD_NOTES.md`, `mecanum_native_profile_pilot02_v1.json`).
  - **CAD wheel handedness note:** inspecting the USD roller axes reveals the raw CAD places `A`
    hubs on `FL`/`RR` and `B` hubs on `FR`/`RL` in an O-on-top / X-on-floor pattern whose yaw moment
    arm is only `|(track - wheelbase)/2| = 27.9 mm`. The drivable prior uses standard top-view
    X-pattern (`mecanum_hand_sign = +1`, yaw moment arm `(track + wheelbase)/2 = 98.7 mm`, matching
    `mecanum_geometry.HANDEDNESS`); verify the physical wheel mounting during characterisation.
  - Sampled worlds select DART's `<solver_type>pgs</solver_type>` because sub-gram TPU rollers
    (`Izz < 1e-8 kg·m²`) trigger an `_dLDLTRemove()` assertion in DART's default Dantzig solver.

## Unknown hardware parameters (DO NOT transfer Freenove values)

Everything below is **UNKNOWN** until the physical payload car is bench-characterised (tracked in
`research/tasks/2026-11-27-characterise-payload-car.md`). Because Gazebo silently assigns a `1 kg`
default mass to any dynamic link that omits `<inertial>`, `payload_car_model.py` emits
`<static>true</static>` (a geometry/visual and collision model only) and intentionally omits
`<inertial>`, motor drive plugins, odometry plugins, camera sensors, and IMU sensors when
unmeasured rather than fabricating values or copying Freenove fits:

- **Mass & inertia:** total mass, chassis mass, single-wheel mass, centre of mass, inertia tensor
  (`UNMEASURED_CHASSIS_MASS_KG_TODO` / `UNMEASURED_WHEEL_MASS_KG_TODO` in `payload_car_model.py`).
- **Drive & motor dynamics:** exact N20 gear ratio, stall torque, no-load RPM, deadzone, PWM-to-duty
  curve, TPU 7-roller friction/lateral slip.
- **Electronics & motor driver:** SBC identity/OS (`PCB_56x41`), motor driver IC/bus/pinout,
  current limits, battery voltage telemetry, hardware watchdog path.
- **Camera:** sensor model behind `M12_lens__1_`, resolution/FPS, rolling vs global shutter,
  calibrated intrinsics (`fx`, `fy`, `cx`, `cy`, distortion).
- **IMU:** BNO055 bus/address, operating mode (raw vs NDOF fusion), mounting matrix, resting noise
  and bias drift.
